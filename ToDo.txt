## Snake Reloaded

---

### Gameplay

* Add configurable game speed and group related settings.
* Make walls visible (with configurable map size).
* Allow snakes to die when they collide with another snake or a wall.
* Enemy snakes should also be able to grow.
* Make AI snakes smarter, with selectable difficulty levels.

| AI        | Behavior                        |
| --------- | ------------------------------- |
| Random    | Moves randomly                  |
| Easy      | Heads directly for apples       |
| Normal    | Avoids obstacles                |
| Hard      | Sets traps                      |
| Expert    | Predicts future moves           |
| Nightmare | Cooperates with other AI snakes |

* Allow configuring how many points are required for each level-up.

---

### Visuals

* Generate a different background for every map.
* Animate lakes with water waves.
* Animate tree canopies from a top-down perspective.
* Subtle background animations are sufficient:

  * flowing water
  * moving leaves
  * clouds
  * birds
* This will make the game world feel more alive.

---

### Birds / Golden Cookie Mechanic

Birds can be caught to earn an extra perk, similar to the Golden Cookies in idle games.

Occasionally a bird flies across the map (for the console version it could simply be a blinking **V** character).

If the player catches the moving bird with the snake's head, the perk selection screen immediately appears, allowing the player to choose an extra perk outside of the normal level-up process.

This creates a high risk/high reward decision, since chasing the bird can easily result in crashing into a wall.

* Bird appears every 2–4 minutes.
* Bird blinks and chirps.
* Configurable spawn interval:

  * 0 = Never
  * 1 minute
  * 2 minutes
  * etc.

### Title Screen

* Animate the title art with color waves.
* Improve the title art.

### Level-Up Perks

Every level-up should reward the player with a perk.

---

## 1. Offensive & Trap Perks

Since snakes now die when colliding with each other or walls, combat-oriented perks become much more interesting.

### Spiky Tail

**Effect**

If an enemy snake's head collides with your body or tail, the enemy takes damage or dies instead of you.

Excellent defensive ability when surrounded.

---

### Poison Trail

**Effect**

Periodically (or on key press), your snake leaves behind a poisonous/slowing cloud.

Enemy snakes entering the cloud are slowed, making them easier to trap.

---

### Tail Whip

**Effect**

As a level-up perk or active ability, your snake performs a sweeping tail attack that pushes nearby enemy snakes backward, potentially causing them to crash into walls.

---

### EMP

All enemy snakes lose their AI for 2 seconds.

---

## 2. Movement & Survival Perks

With higher movement speeds and smarter enemies, precise maneuvering becomes essential.

### Ghost Phase

**Effect**

On key press (or once every 10 seconds), your snake becomes intangible for 1.5 seconds.

You can safely pass through:

* your own body
* enemy snakes
* trees

---

### Handbrake

**Effect**

Holding a key slows your snake by 50%, allowing precise turns while enemies continue moving at full speed.

---

### Iron Head

**Effect**

Once per level, survive a collision with a wall or another snake.

The shield breaks after absorbing the hit.

---

### Time Warp

Slow every other snake for 3 seconds.

---

### Poison Trail

Leave a slowing trail behind you.

---

### Berserk

If your snake is the last survivor:

* +40% movement speed

---

## 3. Score & Growth Perks

These perks improve progression toward the next level.

### Lucky Food

Food variants:

* Red apple → +1
* Gold → +3
* Purple → Perk
* Blue → Shield
* Rainbow → Random effect

---

### Apple Magnet

Nearby food is automatically attracted toward your snake.

---

### Metabolism

Collected food only increases snake length by half while still awarding full experience.

Makes self-collisions less likely.

---

### Double Harvest

15% chance that collected food counts as double experience.

---

## 4. Environmental Perks

Because maps feature animated lakes and moving trees, environmental interaction should also affect gameplay.

### Amphibious

Water no longer slows or pushes your snake.

Instead, movement speed in water is increased by 20%.

---

### Woodpecker

Allows your snake to move through tree canopies while enemy snakes must navigate around them.

---

### Chameleon

While hidden beneath trees or in tall grass, intelligent enemy snakes lose track of your scent and move in the opposite direction.

---

## Roguelike Progression

Instead of receiving a random perk after leveling up, the player should choose one from several upgrade options.

The number of available choices should be configurable in the Settings menu.

The selection panel should also display the keyboard shortcut that activates each perk.

---

### UI

* Display perk activation keys in the status bar.

### Persistent AI Progression

Enemy snakes should also collect perks.

Their complete state should be saved.

Each snake should have:

* unique name
* color theme
* AI personality
* starting/favorite perk
* personal statistics
* personal perks
* wins
* deaths

## Cross-Platform Architecture

Create a MAUI version while preserving the Windows console experience.

A Windows console client should remain available, but the game should also run on mobile devices.

```text
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│             Snake.Core             │
│ (Gameplay, AI, Snake Classes, Perks, Save System)      │
│ (Engine, Grid, Entities, AI, Perks, SaveState, Config) │
└───────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────┘
                            │
             ┌──────────────┴──────────────┐
             ▼                             ▼
┌─────────────────────────┐   ┌──────────────────────────┐
│ Console Client (.NET)   │   │ .NET MAUI Client         │
│ ASCII / ANSI Graphics   │   │ GraphicsView (Canvas)    │
│ Keyboard                │   │ Swipe / Virtual D-Pad    │
│ Console.Beep()          │   │ Bluetooth / LAN Host     │
└─────────────────────────┘   └──────────────────────────┘
```

The Core project should contain the complete game logic, AI (including A* pathfinding), perks, save system, and gameplay rules.

Console client:

* ASCII rendering
* Keyboard input
* Console.Beep() sound effects

MAUI client:

* GraphicsView rendering
* 2D effects
* Swipe controls or optional virtual D-Pad
* Bluetooth multiplayer
